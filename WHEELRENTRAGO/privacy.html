PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated: July 22, 2026

info

WheelRentraGo is a student capstone project. This policy explains what data the demo app collects and how it's handled — it's written for transparency during evaluation, not as a substitute for professional legal advice.

01 Information We Collect

CategoryExamples
Account infoName, email address, password (handled by Supabase Auth)
Verification documentsDriver's license / ID images or details you submit for rider/driver verification
Booking & payment infoReservation details, vehicle selection, demo payment form fields (card/e-wallet — not transmitted to any real payment network)
Support & reviewsMessages sent via the Support form, ratings and comments you leave on completed bookings
Usage & device dataLocal session data, cached app files (via Service Worker), simulated location/tracking data shown in the Tracking view

02 How We Use Information

  • To create and manage your account and reservations.
  • To review and approve/reject rider/driver verification submissions.
  • To respond to support tickets you submit.
  • To display booking history, receipts, and account activity back to you.
  • To send booking-related notifications (e.g., confirmation, cancellation) to your registered email.

03 Where Data Is Stored

Account, booking, verification, and support-ticket data are stored in a Supabase-hosted database. Some non-sensitive session data is also cached locally in your browser (localStorage) so the app can work offline as an installable web app.

04 Third-Party Services

The Service relies on a small number of trusted providers to operate core features:

  • Supabase — database, authentication, and file storage.
  • Email delivery provider — used to send booking and account-related notification emails.
  • Google Fonts and the Tailwind CDN — used only to load fonts and styling, not to track you.

We do not sell personal data to advertisers or third parties.

05 Cookies & Local Storage

WheelRentraGo uses browser localStorage (not tracking cookies) to keep you signed in, cache app screens for offline use, and temporarily hold data such as an in-progress booking or a support ticket if it cannot immediately reach the server.

06 Data Retention

Account and booking records are retained for as long as your account is active, or as needed for the demo/evaluation period of this capstone project, after which data may be periodically cleared.

07 Your Rights

  • You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data by contacting us through the Support page.
  • You may delete locally cached data at any time by clearing your browser's site data for this app.

08 Children's Privacy

The Service is not directed at children and is not knowingly used to collect data from anyone under the legal age required to hold a driver's license in their jurisdiction.

09 Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated as the project evolves. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision.

10 Contact

For any privacy-related questions or requests, please reach out through the Support page.
